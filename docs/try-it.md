# Try Pace

Start at [Pace](https://whoop-delta-sable.vercel.app). Try a demo before connecting,
or connect the WHOOP account you use on your phone.

## Your first visit

- **Demo:** explore sample sleep/recovery trends, move the heart-rate slider to
  change a fictional music match, and edit a sample message greeting. This never
  starts playback or sends messages.
- **Connect WHOOP:** authorize on WHOOP, then return to Overview. Your password
  stays with WHOOP. The Account page shows which email is connected.
- **Overview:** see recent metrics and trends, choose a date range, and refresh
  without leaving the dashboard. Music and Morning texts show their setup status.

## Morning texts

1. Preview the format using clearly labeled sample sleep data.
2. Enter an international recipient number and confirm they want the reports.
3. Review the recipient, then choose **Enable morning texts**.

No test text is sent. “Waiting for first sleep update” means the subscription is
active but no report has been requested yet. “Report requested” means the app
requested delivery; confirm receipt on the recipient's phone. Return here to
change the recipient or turn reports off.

The live greeting is configured by the app owner. WHOOP must process a main-sleep
update before the report can be triggered. This is not an instant wake alarm.

## Music

Use Spotify Premium and a device/browser that supports Web Bluetooth. The live
DJ currently cannot pair through iPhone/Safari. Keep your WHOOP nearby and enable
Heart Rate Broadcast in the WHOOP app.

Connect Spotify, choose music to import, pair WHOOP, and start listening. You need
at least two imported songs with BPM data. Open Spotify on your chosen playback
device; clear its queue and disable shuffle, repeat, autoplay, and crossfade.
Play a song or select the first song in Pace, then start the DJ. Keep the Pace tab
visible. Music already queued can still play after you stop the DJ.

## If something gets stuck

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong or missing sleep | Check your connected email under Account, sync your WHOOP phone app, then refresh |
| Sleep still processing | Check again after WHOOP scores the sleep; missing scores are not zero |
| Connection expired | Reconnect WHOOP; return to the feature you were using |
| Delivery setup pending | The app owner must configure messaging; you can still preview your report |
| Music Start is disabled | Read the explanation below Start: finish import or wait for a heart-rate reading |
| Bluetooth unavailable | Use a compatible Bluetooth-capable browser/device |
| Provider refuses access | Ask the app owner to check developer-app access restrictions and callback configuration |

Account provides disconnect and deletion controls. Deletion does not erase your
original WHOOP records or recall messages already sent. See the app's Privacy page
for data handling details.
